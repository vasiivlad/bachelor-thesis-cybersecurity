# Papers collected

I started with papers about intrusion detection, phishing and ransomware. I haven't chosen the final thesis topic, so some sources may not be used in the end.

| Paper | PDF / article | Why I selected it |
|---|---|---|
| Khraisat et al. (2019) – *Survey of intrusion detection systems: techniques, datasets and challenges* | [PDF](https://cybersecurity.springeropen.com/counter/pdf/10.1186/s42400-019-0038-7.pdf) | General introduction to intrusion detection |
| Ring et al. (2019) – *A survey of network-based intrusion detection data sets* | [Preprint PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1903.02460) | Choosing and comparing intrusion detection datasets |
| Sharafaldin et al. (2018) – *Toward Generating a New Intrusion Detection Dataset and Intrusion Traffic Characterization* | [PDF](https://www.scitepress.org/papers/2018/66398/66398.pdf) | Original paper describing the CICIDS2017 dataset |
| Sommer and Paxson (2010) – *Outside the Closed World: On Using Machine Learning for Network Intrusion Detection* | [Author's PDF](https://www.icir.org/robin/papers/oakland10-ml.pdf) | Problems with evaluating intrusion detection models |
| Sahingoz et al. (2019) – *Machine learning based phishing detection from URLs* | [Article](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.eswa.2018.09.029) | Example of phishing URL classification using machine learning |
| Safi and Singh (2023) – *A systematic literature review on phishing website detection techniques* | [Open-access article](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jksuci.2023.01.004) · [PDF](https://www.researchgate.net/profile/Satwinder-Singh-10/publication/367062510_A_Systematic_Literature_Review_on_Phishing_Website_Detection_Techniques/links/661fdc3c66ba7e2359e5eabf/A-Systematic-Literature-Review-on-Phishing-Website-Detection-Techniques.pdf) | Overview of different phishing detection methods |
| Albshaier et al. (2024) – *Earlier Decision on Detection of Ransomware Identification: A Comprehensive Systematic Literature Review* | [PDF](https://www.mdpi.com/2078-2489/15/8/484/pdf) | Ransomware detection techniques and early warning |
| Cen et al. (2024) – *Ransomware early detection: A survey* | [Article](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.comnet.2023.110138) | Other approaches to detecting ransomware early |

The first four papers relate to network intrusion detection, the next two to phishing, and the last two to ransomware. The two Elsevier articles linked only as "Article" may need university access to download the PDF.

Complete citation information for these eight sources is in [references.bib](../references.bib). The notes are in [LITERATURE_NOTES.md](../notes/LITERATURE_NOTES.md).
